What is Stomach Flu?

Stomach flu is a general term used to describe a group of conditions called gastroenteritis that affect the digestive system. Although called stomach flu, the condition is not related to the common flu or influenza. Nevertheless, the term was coined because both can be caused by a virus (although not the same virus).

Stomach flu affects hundreds of NYC residents every year, such as the recent stomach flu 2019 outbreak and is associated with lost productivity. When an individual has stomach flu, they may feel weak and unwell and, in most cases, may need to take a few days off work to recover. Although generally not deadly, stomach flu can be a major inconvenience when contracted.

What are the Types of Stomach Flu?

Stomach flu is identified by the underlying cause. Here are the types of stomach flu and their causes:

Viral stomach flu: Caused by viruses like norovirus, rotavirus, adenovirus, calicivirus, and astrovirus.

Bacterial stomach flu: Caused by bacteria like Campylobacter bacterium.

Parasitic stomach flu: Caused by parasites like Giardia lamblia, Entamoeba histolytica, and Cryptosporidium bacterium.

Noninfectious stomach flu: Can be caused by any number of factors that cause inflammation of the gastrointestinal tract. Some of these factors include drugs such as NSAIDs, toxins produced by some types of bacteria, and poisoning from chemicals such as lead.

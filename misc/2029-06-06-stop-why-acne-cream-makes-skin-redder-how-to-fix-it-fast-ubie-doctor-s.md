Explanation

When you apply acne creams, they can sometimes make your skin redder. This happens because some ingredients in these creams can irritate the skin or cause an allergic reaction, known as contact dermatitis.

[Ubie Doctor's Note](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/6036719520.html)

[Fast Ubie Doctor's Note](https://leanorbit.dns0663.com/)

Possible Reasons for Redness:

Irritation: Acne creams often contain strong ingredients that help fight pimples, but they can also irritate the skin, making it red and sensitive.

[Fast Ubie](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/blog/8746025473.html)

Allergic Reaction: Some people might be allergic to certain ingredients in the cream, leading to redness and discomfort.

What to Do:

Stop Using the Cream: If your skin becomes very red or uncomfortable, stop using the cream and talk to a healthcare professional.

Try a Patch Test: Before using a new acne cream, try a small amount on a different part of your skin to see if it causes redness.

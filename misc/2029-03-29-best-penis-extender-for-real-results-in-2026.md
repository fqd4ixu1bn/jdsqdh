Traction as a method of enlargement

The easiest way to understand penis traction is by thinking of muscle-building. When people lift weights or work out to build muscle, the exercise causes very tiny tears in our muscle tissue. The body repairs these microtears with cell replication.5 This repair process is what builds our muscles.

In essence, this is the concept behind the effectiveness of traction in enlarging the penis. A traction device, such as a penis extender, applies pressure to stretch the tissues of the penis gradually but persistently for several hours at a time. This pressure causes microtears in the tissues of your penis, which the body then repairs similarly to how it repairs and "builds" muscle after a proper workout. Gradually, this translates to growth in penis size.

As the body repairs microtears in the corpora cavernosa — the spongy tissue in the shaft that collects blood — men find their erections gaining noticeable size and strength. Clinical studies confirm that wearing penis extenders produces actual results for men hoping to enlarge their penises.6

Importantly, the growth in size that some men see within the first couple months of using traction-based devices may not be permanent. But studies show that the gains become permanent after using a traction device for months rather than weeks. Once you have used a penis extender for several months, you can stop when satisfied with your size, knowing that the gains are now permanent.

How big can you get?

Are penis extenders safe?

When used as directed, penis extenders are very safe.8 You should follow the instructions on using your device to avoid potential bruising or discomfort. Listening to your body is important, too. Reputable companies explain a process of gradually ramping up tension strength and the number of hours you use the penis extender. Following these programs is a necessary step to avoid any potential bruising.

Tingling and stretching sensations are normal when using a penis extender, but pain and discomfort aren't normal or expected at all. If you encounter this, you should discontinue use immediately and contact the company. As long as you use the penis extender as intended and aren't overly aggressive in how quickly you increase tension, the devices are designed to cause no harm.

Safety in comparison to alternatives

The only other viable alternative to penis extenders at this time is surgery, which carries with it the risk of unintended side effects. Surgery is almost always a last resort in medicine because the risks of complications tend to increase as compared to non-surgical solutions. When there is a non-invasive, non-surgical method of achieving the same desired results, scientists and medical professionals almost always advise against surgery.10

Penile extension surgery is particularly scary. Some procedures involve severing the base of the penis where it is attached to the pubic bone, which allows flaccid penises to hang more significantly. But these surgeries carry various risks, including erectile dysfunction and difficulty with control during intercourse, as the lack of an anchor to the erect penis makes it hard to direct one way or another.

Other procedures, often intended to increase penile girth, involve layering extra tissue beneath the skin of the penis. This tissue sometimes comes from cadavers, and both rejection and infection are noteworthy risks. Alternative methods now exist to implant silicone instead of actual tissue; in the case of silicone implants, the surgical procedure must be reversed due to serious compilation in three percent of operations.

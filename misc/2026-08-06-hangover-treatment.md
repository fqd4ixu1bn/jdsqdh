Causes & Symptoms of Hangovers

Hangovers are caused by the dehydration, vitamin, and nutrient depletion caused by excessive alcohol consumption. The end result is an array of symptoms that can be debilitating. The symptoms of a hangover can last for hours without proper treatment.

Nausea

Muscle aches

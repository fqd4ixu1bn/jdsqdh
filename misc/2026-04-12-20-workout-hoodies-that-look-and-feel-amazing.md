What Makes a Good Workout Hoodie?

Temperature Regulation: All workout hoodies fall into one of two categories, they’re either designed to lock your body heat in or maximize breathability to let it out. Obviously, one of these categories are designed for cold conditions and the other for warm conditions. Wearing a hoodie to workout in the cold is obvious but working out in the cold still requires a degree of breathability. You need to let some body heat out and keep sweat retention to a minimum because a wet, sweat-drenched body will catch a nasty chill as soon as cold air hits it.

There are hoodies designed to reduce body heat, either through breathable fabrics or openings … or the cutting the sleeves off all together. There are also workout hoodies designed for the outdoors and these are based on UV protection. The sun can do nasty things to your body through both short and long term exposure. Sunscreen is great and should be worn wherever possible but even the best sport sunscreen will need to be reapplied. To avoid this hindrance to your workout, a UV protection hoodie that protects your neck and face while also regulating body heat is a great alternative.

Weight and Friction: This one is pretty straight forward. Whether your workout hoodie is designed for warm or cold conditions, you want to keep weight at a minimum. You also want to keep friction at a minimum for both your body running against itself and rubbing against other things. Most of the recent advancements in athletic apparel fabric technology have achieved these two goals. Ultimately, the goal of a good workout hoodie is to feel like you’re not wearing it at all. If it is lightweight enough and doesn’t cause any chafing or snagging, then your workout won’t be impaired.

Special Features: Some workout hoodies are minimalist, cutting down on any unnecessary weight. Others make room for handy special features like secure, tucked away pockets and thumb hole cuffs to keep your sleeves in place. If you’re going to the gym and using a locker then you probably won’t need any special features, you’ll just want a hoodie as minimalist as possible. If you’re going for a run, hiking, biking, or any other activity on the move, then these special features come in handy.

Are women's nipples generally more sensitive than men's, and why?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, women's nipples are typically more sensitive than men's. This heightened sensitivity is primarily driven by hormones like estrogen and oxytocin, along with a higher concentration of nerve endings that support essential reproductive functions such as breastfeeding, arousal, and bonding. Sensitivity levels can also fluctuate throughout the menstrual cycle, during pregnancy, and around menopause.

Vomiting is the body's forceful reflex to expel contents from the stomach. It involves coordinated action between the abdominal muscles, diaphragm, and respiratory system to push stomach contents up and out through the mouth. While occasional vomiting can be harmless, it may also signal an underlying condition such as infection, food poisoning, migraine, or a more serious digestive issue.

Muscle Actions: Muscles in the stomach and the muscles we use to breathe work together. They squeeze and push to help force the contents of the stomach out through the mouth.

A vaginal foreign body is an object retained inside the vagina, which can cause symptoms such as unusual or foul-smelling discharge, unexpected bleeding, pelvic pain, or discomfort. Common causes include a forgotten tampon, contraceptive device, or other item. Prompt medical evaluation is important, as untreated cases may lead to infection or complications.

A vaginal foreign body is something that is not supposed to be in the vagina and can cause different symptoms. Some common signs include unusual discharge, which may have a bad smell, and bleeding that is not related to a menstrual period. There can also be discomfort or pain in the vaginal area.

Here are some possible symptoms of a vaginal foreign body:

Unusual Discharge: This discharge might have a bad smell and be a different color than normal.

Bleeding: Bleeding can happen and might not be related to your regular period.

Discomfort or Pain: You might feel pain or discomfort in the vaginal area.

If you notice any of these symptoms, it is important to see a healthcare provider. They can help find out what is causing the symptoms and how to treat it.

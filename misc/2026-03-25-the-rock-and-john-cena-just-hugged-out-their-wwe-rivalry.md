# The Rock and John Cena Just Hugged Out Their WWE Rivalry

The ongoing SAG-AFTRA strike may mean that production on all current Hollywood projects is suspended, but for many wrestling fans there is a silver lining to that cloud: two hulking action stars now have some unexpected free time to return to their origins.

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson attended the WWE SmackDown! event on Friday night in Denver, Colorado, and was interviewed backstage by talk show host and internet personality Pat McAfee. Johnson famously comes from a family of pro wrestlers, and was a huge star in the WWE before making the transition to movies.

But Johnson wasn't the only wrestler-turned-actor in the building: John Cena, who has made a name for himself in DC's Suicide Squad and Peacemaker, made a surprise appearance too.

As their conversation was coming to an end, McAfee pointed behind Johnson, saying: "Hey, there's a guy, he can't see you," referring to Cena's wrestling catchphrase "you can't see me."

The audience erupted as the camera panned to reveal Cena, and the two stood in a stony-faced standoff for a moment. Johnson and Cena were, of course, longtime enemies during their WWE heyday, frequently taking their rivalry to the ring and fighting it out. But there were to be no showdowns this time.

"I see you trying to smile," said Johnson, and then both he and Cena broke, shaking hands and hugging it out, officially burying the long-held hatchet after all these years. After Fast X declined to show the two men on-screen together, it was the reunion that fans were beginning to think might never happen.

"Welcome home," said Cena.

"Thank you," said Johnson. "Good to be here."

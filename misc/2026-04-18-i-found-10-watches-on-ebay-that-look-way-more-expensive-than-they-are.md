As Men's Health editors, we swear by the Apple Watch, and wear it to work and workout in. But let's be honest; it doesn't exactly complete an outfit. There's where a real watch comes in. Something with weight. Something with history. Something you'd actually want to wear with your nicest summer suit. And when it comes to finding a storied watch? ebay is one of the smartest places to look. As a menswear editor, I've scoured eBay to find styles that aren't made anymore, vintage models with tons of character, and sold-out collabs you won't see on any other guy's wrist. Plus, shopping via eBay is usually more affordable than buying retail, and with the site's Authenticity Guarantee—where experts physically inspect watches before they're shipped—you know what you're getting is actually legit.

We tapped eBay's in-house stylist Brie Welch—who has worked with Bulgari, Ferragamo, and Givenchy—to find the best-of-the-best-timepieces that deliver great value and play well with everything in your closet. “Watch buying is personal,” says Welch. “It’s about finding exactly what you want—and being able to search that specifically is what makes eBay so useful.” she breaks down the best watches on eBay right now, from under-$300 pieces to splurge-worthy investments you’ll wear for life.

Bulova Sutton Classic

If you’re just getting into watches, or just want a dependable everyday piece, the Bulova Sutton is a no-brainer. “Bulovas are always a popular choice on eBay,” Welch says. This classic style delivers sleek looks, and a range of dial and strap options so you can personalize it to your style. It’s affordable, easy to wear, and cool enough to make people think you paid more than you did.

Timex x The Noah Sun And Moon Watch

This collab was an instant sell-out for a reason. “It just looks elevated on the wrist,” says Welch. The charming sun-and-moon dial feels nostalgic in the best way, while the clean design means you can wear this on the regular. If you want to stand out without spending a fortune, this is your guy.

Rolex GMT Master II

Want a pricey watch that's actually worth the investment? “A 60s or 70s GMT-Master will never go out of style,” says Welch. This a ticker that gets passed down because it’s instantly recognizable and built to last. Whether you’re a collector or just looking to upgrade your wrist game, this is a top-tier pick.

Cartier Tank

“The Tank is the definition of timeless,” Welch says, and she’s right. It’s slim, polished, and instantly dressed up a fit. Originally designed in 1917, its clean, slim face hasn't changed much in over a century, and it still looks as good with a tux as it does with your casual Friday uniform.

Tudor Black Bay

This one hits a rare style trifecta: rugged, classy, and versatile. "It works just as well with jeans as it does a blazer,” Welch says. No wonder the Black Bay has become one of eBay’s top-selling watches. Think of it as your one-watch wardrobe upgrade.

Piaget Polo

Want something with heritage and personality? “A vintage Piaget Polo is unmistakably unique,” says Welch. This 80s-era ticker is loaded with gold tones and a sporty feel that's back in style. It’s nostalgic without trying too hard, and definitely not something you’ll see on every other wrist.

Omega Seamaster

[Look Way](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

“This watch is having a major moment,” Welch says. It hits the right note between function and flash, with stopwatch capabilities, a striking dial, and seriously durable wristband. It’s one of the top-selling luxury watches on eBay this year, which means it’s both a collector’s piece and a conversation starter. Great for guys who like to geek out over details and design.

Seiko Half Moon Watch

Search for this watch is up 430% this year on eBay. "It's a breakout star," says Welch. The Seiko Half Moon has a curved case (living up to its name), and just enough funk to make it cool. Seiko's awesomely weird watches always have style cred, and this one proves why.

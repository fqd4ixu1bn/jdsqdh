# Pre & Post Treatment Care

Pre & Post Treatment Care

Post-Treatment

IV Therapy – Pre-Treatment Instructions

Required Before Treatment

A current health history and assessment must be on file (updated within the last 12 months)

Medical history changes

Current medications and supplements

Allergies

Cardiac, kidney, or liver conditions

Risk of fluid overload

Pregnancy or breastfeeding status

Baseline vital signs will be taken prior to your infusion

[Pre Post Treatment](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/blog/5925252828.html)

Before Your Appointment

Eat a light meal prior to your visit

Stay well hydrated

Avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior

Wear comfortable clothing with easy access to your arms

IV Therapy - Post-Treatment Instructions

[Pre Post](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

What to Expect

During or after your infusion, you may experience:

A cooling sensation in your arm

A feeling of fullness from fluids

Increased urination

These symptoms are typically short-lived and resolve within 1–3 days.

Dizziness or lightheadedness

Headache

Fatigue

Minor bruising or soreness at the IV site

These are typically temporary and resolve quickly.

Infusion Overview (What You Received)

Your IV infusion may include:

Fluids (Normal Saline or Lactated Ringer’s)

Vitamins and nutrients to support energy, immunity, or recovery

[Pre Post](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Add-ons such as vitamin C or glutathione depending on your treatment

Infusions are typically completed over 45–90 minutes depending on the formulation.

IV Site Care

Mild soreness or bruising is normal

[Post Treatment Care](https://ageless-peptides.china-akan.com/)

Keep the area clean and avoid excessive pressure

You may apply a cold compress if needed

Dosing & Titration Schedule

Please contact the office if you experience:

Persistent dizziness or weakness

Swelling, pain, or redness at the IV site that worsens

[Post Treatment Care](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/5604713573.html)

Fever or signs of infection

Rash or allergic symptoms

Seek emergency care for severe reactions (difficulty breathing, significant swelling, chest pain, etc.).

Important Considerations

IV hydration is intended for wellness support only

[Pre Post](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/3762381834.html)

It does not replace medical treatment for underlying conditions

Your provider may adjust your treatment based on your response and medical history

How to Feel Your Best After Treatment

[Post Treatment Care](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

Continue to hydrate throughout the day

Eat balanced meals

Avoid alcohol for the remainder of the day

Resume normal activity as tolerated

Follow-Up & Maintenance

Treatments can be repeated based on your goals and provider recommendations

Your response, tolerance, and overall wellness will guide future treatments

Medical Disclaimer: This post‑care information is provided for general guidance and does not replace professional medical evaluation or treatment. If you experience symptoms that may indicate a serious or potentially life‑threatening condition, seek immediate medical care.

Chris Evans: Was a no-show at the Infinity War premiere

Some of your favorite Marvel superheroes made their way to Los Angeles on Monday for the premiere of Avengers: Infinity War — but Chris Evans was not among them.

If Twitter is any indication, Captain America fans were not pleased to see that Evan skipped out on walking the purple carpet.

Apparently Evans had better things to do — namely, performing on Broadway.

“So sorry I couldn’t be at the premiere last night!!

Overweight and obesity develop when the body stores excess fat, most often due to consuming more calories than are burned through daily activity. While overeating and a sedentary lifestyle are the leading causes, other factors—including genetics, hormones, medications, sleep patterns, stress, and certain medical conditions—can also contribute.

Explanation

Being overweight or obese happens when we eat more calories than our bodies use. This can happen if we eat a lot of unhealthy foods and do not exercise enough.

Main Causes:

[Know Doctor's Note Ubie](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/6493177322.html)

Unhealthy Eating Habits: Eating lots of fast food, sugary drinks, and snacks can lead to gaining too much weight.

Lack of Physical Activity: Not moving around or exercising enough means our bodies don't burn off the calories we eat.

Genetics: Sometimes, people can gain weight more easily because of their genes.

Other Factors: Things like lack of sleep, stress, and certain medicines can also make it easier to gain weight.

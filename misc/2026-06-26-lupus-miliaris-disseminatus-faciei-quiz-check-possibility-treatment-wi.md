What is Lupus Miliaris Disseminatus Faciei?

Lupus miliaris disseminatus faciei is a rare skin condition characterized by red-yellow or yellow-brown patches on the central face, especially around the eyelids. The cause is unknown but could be due to tuberculosis, a foreign body reaction (particularly to zirconium), and so on.

Diagnostic Questions for Lupus Miliaris Disseminatus Faciei

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have pus-filled blisters or bumps on your skin?

Do you have small goose bump-like bumps on your skin?

Did your skin issues start on the face?

Do you have any skin problems

Have you noticed any redness, bumps, or irritation on your skin?

Treatment of Lupus Miliaris Disseminatus Faciei

Treatment involves medication to suppress inflammation, usually in a combination of oral and topical therapies.

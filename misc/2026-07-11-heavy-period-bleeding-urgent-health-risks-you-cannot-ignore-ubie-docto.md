Heavy menstrual bleeding (menorrhagia) is more than an inconvenience — it can significantly reduce quality of life and lead to serious health concerns, including iron deficiency, anemia, chronic fatigue, and weakness. Common signs include soaking through pads or tampons every hour, passing large clots, or periods lasting longer than seven days. Causes range from hormonal imbalances and fibroids to thyroid conditions or bleeding disorders.

Heavy menstrual bleeding can have significant health effects. It can make daily life harder and lower the quality of life because it might cause tiredness and limit activities.

Health Implications:

Iron Deficiency: Losing a lot of blood can lead to low iron levels, which is important for making red blood cells.

Anemia: If iron levels drop too much, it can cause anemia, making a person feel very tired and weak.

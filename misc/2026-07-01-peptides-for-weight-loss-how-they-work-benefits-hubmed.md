What Are Peptides?

Peptides are tiny chains of amino acids, which are the building blocks of proteins. Think of peptides as tiny messengers that tell different parts of your body what to do. Unlike complete proteins, peptides are shorter and easier for the body to absorb and use.

Peptide Injections vs. Oral Peptides: Which is More Effective for Weight Loss?

Peptides for weight loss can be taken as injections or oral supplements, each with pros and cons. The right choice depends on absorption, effectiveness, and convenience.

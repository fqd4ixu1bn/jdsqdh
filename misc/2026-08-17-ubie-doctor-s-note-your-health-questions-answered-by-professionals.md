In women, these episodes are more likely during stress, sleep deprivation, or hormonal shifts such as pregnancy, postpartum, perimenopause, or menopause. **When to be concerned:** Watch for red flags including voices while fully awake, worsening or threatening content, confusion, mood changes, neurological symptoms, or recent medication changes. These warrant prompt medical evaluation. **What helps:** Improving sleep hygiene, managing stress, and addressing hormonal changes often reduce these episodes.

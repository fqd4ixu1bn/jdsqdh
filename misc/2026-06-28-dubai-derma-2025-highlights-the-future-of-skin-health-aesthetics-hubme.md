Tackling Hyperpigmentation: Smarter Solutions for Even-Toned Skin

Hyperpigmentation continues to challenge patients and professionals alike—especially in skin of color. This year, Dubai Derma brought forward exciting innovations in laser technologies, pigment regulators, targeted topical and regenerative therapies that work on a cellular level.

The emerging trend? Combination treatments. Experts showcased powerful duos like exosomes with microneedling, and melasyl paired with laser—each showing promising results in reducing melasma and photoaging. Sessions also explored peptide serums and DNA repair boosters as preventative tools, offering long-term strategies for brighter, healthier skin.

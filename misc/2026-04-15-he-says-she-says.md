Look 1: Vest, tie, slacks

He says: "I misjudged these clothes when I saw them on the rack. The wool vest seemed stuffy, the slacks dull—I pictured them hoisted above my navel. But I was wrong. The combo felt great. With a couple of sharp details—the tie bar and pocket square—the look seemed commanding. Would I wear this to meet buds for dollar drafts at our favorite dive? Not likely. It doesn't slum well. But it could help me hold a woman's attention in a shadowy cocktail bar. Some nights, that's plenty."

She says: "My first thought: pompous finance guy who's trying too hard. But I know other women who would swoon for this. It's a great outfit, but one that can be made or broken by the guy who's wearing it and by the occasion."

Look 2: Sweater, chinos

He says: "If I wanted to be this comfortable, I'd have worn pajamas. Walking across the room, I could feel the soft wool sweater shifting across my shoulders. Look 3: T-shirt, jacket, jeans

He says: "This is closest to what I'd wear for a night out with friends. It's a safe play: Jeans almost never fail, and as long as the T-shirt isn't splashed with Ed Hardy graffiti or something equally gaudy, it simplifies pre-bar strategizing. It's a look that transitions most naturally from the cocktail lounge to the dive bar and, after bars close, to the diner or afterparty. I'd dress like this if I didn't know where the night would take me—and quite often, those are the best nights." (Another option? These New Blues.)

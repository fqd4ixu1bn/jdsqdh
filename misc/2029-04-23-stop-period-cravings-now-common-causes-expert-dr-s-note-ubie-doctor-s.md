What are common food cravings during a period?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[Causes Expert Dr's](https://980187.com/f3g0dsbt/)

Why do women crave chocolate, sweets, salty snacks, and carbs during their period? These common menstrual cravings are driven by hormonal shifts—particularly drops in estrogen and serotonin—that trigger the body's need for quick energy, mood-boosting foods, and comfort.

Chocolate cravings may reflect low magnesium levels, while salt and carb cravings can signal fluid retention and blood sugar dips tied to the luteal phase of your cycle. While cravings are usually normal, intense or disruptive symptoms could point to conditions like PMS, PMDD, or hormonal imbalances worth investigating.

During your period, it's common to experience cravings for specific foods. One of the most frequently mentioned cravings is for chocolate. Chocolate is popular because it can boost serotonin levels, which may help improve your mood during a time when hormone levels are shifting. Along with chocolate, many women also find themselves wanting sweet treats like candies or desserts. Salty snacks such as chips or pretzels and carbohydrate-rich foods like bread or pasta are common too. These foods can make you feel comforted and provide quick energy, which can be especially appealing when you're dealing with the discomfort or lower energy levels that sometimes come with menstruation. Research suggests that these cravings arise from a combination of hormonal fluctuations, nutritional needs, and even psychological factors. While enjoying your favorite treats is perfectly normal, aim for balance by including a variety of nutrient-dense foods in your diet to help maintain overall health during your menstrual cycle. If these cravings ever seem overwhelming or interfere with your daily life, you can check your symptoms to better understand what your body might be experiencing and consider discussing them with a healthcare provider or nutritionist for additional guidance.

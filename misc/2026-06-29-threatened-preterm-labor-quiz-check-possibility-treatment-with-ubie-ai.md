What is Threatened Preterm Labor?

This refers to regular contractions that occur between 22 to 37 weeks of pregnancy. There may be associated cervical widening and softening, and it has a risk of preterm delivery.

Typical Symptoms of Threatened Preterm Labor

Feel my child is moving less. (Decreased fetal movements)

Currently pregnant

Feel like water has broken/amniotic fluid is coming out

Vaginal bleeding or discolored discharge outside of your period

Stomach or abdominal pain accompanied by a hard abdomen

Feeling contractions very frequently

Stomach or abdominal pain

Pain in the lower abdomen

Diagnostic Questions for Threatened Preterm Labor

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Is your child moving less?

Are you pregnant?

Do you feel like your water has broken?

Have you experienced any vaginal bleeding or unusual discharge outside of your period?

Are you experiencing stomach or abdominal pain with a hard abdomen?

Treatment of Threatened Preterm Labor

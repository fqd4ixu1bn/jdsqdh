# Raw Egg Salmonella Risk: Urgent Warning Before You Eat! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

What are the risks of consuming raw eggs in terms of salmonella infection?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[Warning Before You](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Raw eggs may contain Salmonella, a type of bacteria that can cause food poisoning. Symptoms often include diarrhea, stomach cramps, fever, nausea, and vomiting, typically starting 6 hours to 6 days after exposure. While many people recover within a week, Salmonella infection can be severe—or even life-threatening—for young children, older adults, pregnant individuals, and those with weakened immune systems.

If you've eaten raw eggs and are feeling unwell, it's important to understand your symptoms early. Signs like persistent vomiting, high fever, bloody stools, or dehydration may signal a serious infection that needs prompt medical attention. Because symptoms can overlap with other illnesses, getting clarity quickly matters.

What is Fournier's Gangrene?

A potentially life-threatening infection of the genital area that spreads quickly and can destroy cells of muscles, nerves, and blood vessels. Harmful microorganisms often enter through a break in the skin. It is most common in older men but can affect anyone. Risk factors include diabetes, weak immune system, alcohol abuse, obesity, etc.

Typical Symptoms of Fournier's Gangrene

Pain in the region between the scrotum and the anus

Pain in my penis

Fever

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Redness in the affected area

Areas of irritated or peeling skin

History of diabetes or pre-diabetes

Swelling in the affected area

Treatment of Fournier's Gangrene

The infection can spread to other parts of the body, so it is crucial to seek prompt medical attention. Immediate treatment involves surgical removal of infected and dead tissue, wound care, and aggressive treatment with antibiotics injected into the vein.

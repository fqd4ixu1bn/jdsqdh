How long does it typically take for a sprained ankle to heal completely?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A sprained ankle typically heals in 2 to 12 weeks, though severe sprains may take several months to fully recover. Mild (Grade 1) sprains often improve within 1–3 weeks, moderate (Grade 2) sprains in 3–6 weeks, and severe (Grade 3) sprains can require 3–6 months of healing and rehabilitation.

Healing time for a sprained ankle can vary widely. According to the studies provided, mild sprains may heal within a few weeks, while more severe sprains can take several months.

Factors Influencing Healing Time:

Severity of the Sprain:

Mild sprains may heal faster, often within 2-4 weeks.

Severe sprains might take longer, sometimes up to 12 weeks or more.

Treatment and Care:

Proper care, such as rest, ice, compression, and elevation (often referred to as RICE), can help speed up recovery.

Persistent Issues:

Some people may experience persistent issues or disability even after the initial healing period, which can extend the recovery time.

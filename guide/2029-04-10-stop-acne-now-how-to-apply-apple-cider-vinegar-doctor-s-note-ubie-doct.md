Explanation

Apple cider vinegar (ACV) is often used for acne treatment because it contains acetic acid, which has antibacterial properties. This means it can help kill bacteria that might cause acne. The vinegar's acidity can also help balance the skin's pH, potentially reducing oiliness and preventing further breakouts.

How to Apply Apple Cider Vinegar:

Dilute the Vinegar: Mix one part apple cider vinegar with three parts water. This helps prevent irritation, as vinegar is very strong when undiluted.

Apply to Skin: Use a cotton ball to gently apply the mixture to the affected areas of the skin.

Leave it On: Let it sit on your skin for about 5 to 20 seconds, then rinse it off with water.

Several biochemical pathways help explain Shilajit's effects:

Mitochondrial SupportFulvic acid appears to enhance mitochondrial function, improving cellular energy (ATP) production. Better energy output can translate to improved stamina and metabolic health.

Evidence in Aging and Longevity

While human data remain limited, several preclinical and small-scale clinical studies are encouraging:

A rodent study showed enhanced learning and memory in aging rats supplemented with Shilajit, likely via antioxidant effects.

In a randomized, double-blind trial of healthy volunteers (ages 45–60), 250 mg of purified Shilajit twice daily for 90 days improved fatigue scores and certain cognitive measures.

In vitro research indicates protection of endothelial cells (lining blood vessels) from oxidative damage, suggesting cardiovascular benefits.

Though promising, more large-scale human trials are needed before declaring Shilajit a miracle molecule. Always view it as one component of a broader longevity protocol.

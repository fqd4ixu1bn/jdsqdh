Explanation

Turmeric is a spice that contains curcumin, which may help with arthritis symptoms like pain and swelling. Curcumin is thought to work by reducing inflammation in the body.

Benefits of Turmeric for Arthritis:

Pain Relief: Studies suggest that curcumin can help reduce pain in people with arthritis.

Reduced Swelling: It may also help decrease swelling, which is common in arthritis.

Recommended Dosage:

Curcumin Dosage: The amount of curcumin often recommended is between 500 to 1,000 mg per day. It's important to note that curcumin is not easily absorbed by the body, so some supplements add black pepper to help with absorption.

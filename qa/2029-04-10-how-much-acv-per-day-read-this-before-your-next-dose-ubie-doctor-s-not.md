Apple cider vinegar (ACV) has a recommended daily intake of 1 to 2 tablespoons (15–30 mL), typically diluted in a large glass of water. This modest amount is linked to potential health benefits, including improved blood sugar regulation, better mood, appetite control, and support for digestion. Exceeding this amount may cause side effects such as tooth enamel erosion, throat irritation, or digestive discomfort.

# Is it RSV or a Cold? Don’t Wait: Spot These Vital Signs Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

How can you tell the difference between a common cold and RSV?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

The common cold and RSV (respiratory syncytial virus) share many symptoms, including runny nose, cough, sneezing, and mild fever. However, RSV is typically more serious and often causes lower respiratory tract issues such as wheezing, rapid breathing, and shortness of breath. These severe symptoms are especially common in infants, young children, and adults over 65, who face a higher risk of complications like bronchiolitis or pneumonia.

Because symptoms can overlap, it's easy to mistake one for the other—delaying care when it matters most. If you or a loved one is experiencing respiratory symptoms, don't guess.

Published on: 7/14/2026

Are there any treatments for simultaneous face burning and tingling?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Treatment for face burning and tingling depends on the underlying cause. When trigeminal nerve issues are involved, options may include medications, nerve blocks, surgery, or specialized procedures such as microvascular decompression or radiofrequency ablation to relieve symptoms. Other causes—like shingles, multiple sclerosis, vitamin deficiencies, anxiety, or sinus infections—require very different approaches, so an accurate diagnosis is essential before starting any treatment plan.

Because facial burning and tingling can stem from so many conditions—some minor, some serious—guessing at the cause can delay the right care. A free, instant, online symptom check can help you quickly narrow down likely causes based on your specific symptoms, giving you clearer next steps and helping you decide whether self-care, a primary care visit, or urgent evaluation is appropriate.

Burning and tingling in the face can be caused by common things like an irritant (ie: lotion, makeup, perfume). It may also be related to something less common like an issue with the trigeminal nerve. If related to trigeminal neuralgia, there are a few treatments that might help with these symptoms:

Surgical Options: One treatment is called microvascular decompression, which can help relieve symptoms by addressing the pressure on the nerve (Reference 2).

Gamma Knife Surgery: This is a type of radiation treatment that can also help with nerve issues, but it might cause some problems like nerve dysfunction (Reference 3).

Understanding and Diagnosis: It's important to figure out if the symptoms are due to trigeminal neuralgia or neuropathy, as this will guide the treatment choice (Reference 1).

These treatments aim to reduce the symptoms and improve quality of life. If you're experiencing facial burning or tingling, you can check your symptoms with a free AI-powered assessment to better understand what might be causing your discomfort and whether you should seek medical care.

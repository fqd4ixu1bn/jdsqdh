Burning urination, also known as dysuria, is a common symptom in men that can be associated with the presence of pus in the urethra. This condition can occur for various reasons, but infections are a primary cause.

Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs): STIs, such as gonorrhea and chlamydia, are common causes of burning urination and pus discharge in men. These infections can lead to inflammation of the urethra, causing discomfort and discharge.

Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Although less common in men compared to women, UTIs can also cause burning urination and pus. Certain groups, such as sexually active homosexual men, may be at higher risk for UTIs.

It is important for men experiencing these symptoms to seek medical evaluation to determine the underlying cause and receive appropriate treatment. Early diagnosis and management are crucial to prevent complications.

Where can I find dress shirts to fit my large chest and narrow waist?

Answer:

The V shape looks great with the right clothes to show it off (subtly, of course). Brooks Brothers and other men's stores offer custom-tailored shirts. A cheaper option is a dress shirt that has some stretch to it. Perry Ellis, Geoffrey Beene, and Theory all make great shirts; look for a blend of cotton and 2 percent Lycra. Your best bet, however, is to buy the shirt for your chest and have it tailored for your waist. Most tailors will charge $20, which saves hundreds compared with custom shirts and lets you stick with a fabric and brand you're already comfortable with.

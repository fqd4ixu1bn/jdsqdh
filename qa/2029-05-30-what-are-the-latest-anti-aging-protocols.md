Testosterone Therapy

Testosterone is the male hormone that gives a man ‘masculinity’. It is produced in the testicles and is responsible for building muscle mass, strong bones, sexual desire, and sperm production.

Studies show that starting at the age of 30 men naturally lose about 1% of testosterone each year. Other than age, testosterone levels can decline as a result of disease, environmental factors such as smoking, sedentary lifestyles, and an unhealthy diet.

Declining testosterone levels present challenges such as loss of muscle mass, low libido, lack of energy and strength, and a build-up of body fat. While these issues are all physical, they can have compounding psychological effects leading to low self-esteem and depression.

This combination of factors can quickly lead to chronic illnesses like diabetes and heart disease. This not only lowers the quality of life but accelerates the aging process.

Human Growth Hormone (HGH)

The human growth hormone is produced in the pituitary gland and is responsible for growth in children and adolescents. It also plays a role in other bodily functions such as bone and muscle growth and regulating sugar and fat metabolism. HGH also boosts cell regeneration and growth and speeds up muscle repair after injury. The production of HGH naturally declines with age.

A synthetic form of HGH was developed in 1985 and approved by the FDA for treating growth-related disorders. HGH is used to combat the effects of aging due to its ability to regenerate and repair cells. It also increases muscle growth, energy, sex drive, memory, and vision.

But how exactly does Epitalon work? This peptide increases the natural production of an enzyme called telomerase. Telomerase aids body cells in the production of telomeres, the protective parts of DNA. Aging shortens telomeres, limiting their ability to grow and multiply. By supporting the creation of telomers, Epitalon protects our DNA, giving it the chance to replicate, grow new cells, and rejuvenate old ones. This effectively reverses the aging process.

The most effective method of administering Epitalon is via intravenous or intramuscular injection. Nasal sprays and skin applications are also available, but these are not as effective.

About the Symptom

Also known as globus sensation, it describes the feeling that something is stuck inside the throat area. It can be due to postnasal drip, inflammation of the pharynx or esophagus, or something that is or was stuck in the throat.

Doctor's Diagnostic Questions

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this symptom:

Do you feel that an object is stuck in your throat?

How long have you had the feeling of something stuck in your throat?

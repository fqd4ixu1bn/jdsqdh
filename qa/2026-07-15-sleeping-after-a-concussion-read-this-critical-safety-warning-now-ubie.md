When to seek emergency care: Get immediate medical attention if you notice severe warning signs such as repeated vomiting, seizures, inability to wake up, slurred speech, or trouble moving limbs.

Explanation

A concussion is a form of traumatic brain injury and it can range from mild to severe. If you or someone you know may have a concussion, it is important to seek medical care -- and it is especially important to seek medical care immediately in a hospital if there are any of the following symptoms:

Some Reasons To Seek Urgent Medical Care For Someone With A Concussion:

Any symptoms that are worsening, such as headache

Nausea or vomiting

Confusion that is not improving

Loss of consciousness

Unable to wake up

Seizure

Leg or arm weakness or numbness

[Sleeping After Concussion Read](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/1104259737.html)

Difficulty making eye contact, walking, or speaking

After a healthcare provider determines it is safe to recover from a concussion at home (rather than in the hospital), getting enough sleep is part of how someone recovers from a concussion. It is still important to monitor symptoms at home, in case any symptoms or behaviors worsen.

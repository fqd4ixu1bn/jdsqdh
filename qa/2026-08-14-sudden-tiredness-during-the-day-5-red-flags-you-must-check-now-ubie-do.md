Explanation

Sudden tiredness during the day can happen for several reasons. One possible cause is neurological issues, which can lead to excessive daytime sleepiness and fatigue. This means that problems with the brain or nerves might make you feel very sleepy during the day, even if you slept well at night.

Another reason for feeling tired suddenly could be a lack of vitamin B12. Vitamin B12 is important for your body to work well, and not having enough of it can make you feel sleepy and tired during the day.

[Flags You](https://benpepsi.com/)

Lastly, other medical factors can also cause you to feel sleepy suddenly. For example, some people might feel very sleepy when driving, which can be dangerous. This could be due to different health issues that affect how well you sleep and how awake you feel during the day.

If you find yourself feeling tired often during the day, it might be helpful to check your symptoms to better understand what might be causing your fatigue and determine if you should see a doctor.

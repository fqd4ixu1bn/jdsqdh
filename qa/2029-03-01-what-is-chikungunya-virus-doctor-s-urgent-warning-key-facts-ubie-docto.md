Chikungunya is a mosquito-borne viral illness that typically causes sudden high fever and severe joint pain, often accompanied by muscle aches, headache, rash, and fatigue. Spread primarily by Aedes mosquitoes, the virus is most common in tropical and subtropical regions, though outbreaks have occurred worldwide. While rarely fatal, joint pain can persist for weeks or months.

Because Chikungunya symptoms overlap with dengue, Zika, and other illnesses, identifying the cause early matters. Explanation

Chikungunya virus is a mosquito-borne virus that primarily causes fever and joint pain. It is transmitted to humans through the bites of infected mosquitoes, mainly the Aedes aegypti and Aedes albopictus species. These mosquitoes are also known to spread other diseases like dengue and Zika.

Symptoms:

Fever: Sudden onset of high fever is common.

Joint Pain: Severe joint pain, often in the hands and feet, can last for weeks or months.

Other Symptoms: Some people may also experience headache, muscle pain, rash, or fatigue.

To prevent Chikungunya, it's important to avoid mosquito bites by using insect repellent, wearing long sleeves and pants, and staying in places with screens or air conditioning.

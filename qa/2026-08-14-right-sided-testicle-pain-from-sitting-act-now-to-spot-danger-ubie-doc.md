Explanation

Testicle pain can be uncomfortable and concerning. While sitting too long or wearing tight clothing might contribute to discomfort, it is important to look at other possible reasons for the pain.

Chronic Testicular Pain: This type of pain can last for a long time and may not always have a clear cause. It's important to see a healthcare provider to rule out serious conditions.

Other Causes: Testicle pain can also be caused by infections, injuries, or other medical conditions. If the pain is severe or doesn't go away, it is important to seek medical attention.

In summary, while lifestyle factors like sitting or clothing can play a role, chronic testicular pain often requires a more comprehensive look to identify the underlying cause.

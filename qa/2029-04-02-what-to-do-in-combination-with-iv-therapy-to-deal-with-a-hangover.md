How Does IV Therapy Help With a Hangover?

When you have a hangover, the body becomes dehydrated and loses many electrolytes, other minerals, and nutrients needed to function correctly. IV therapy can help treat various conditions, and you can also get an IV drip specifically designed to treat hangovers. It works by restoring the essential vitamins and minerals your body needs and providing hydration simultaneously so that you start to feel better and some symptoms can decrease. Other Tips to Deal With a Hangover

There are several simple steps you can take to help eal with your hangover in addition to IV therapy.

Rehydrate

Anyone in medicine will tell you that dehydration is one of the main culprits behind how you feel when you have a hangover. Alcohol gradually reduces the amount of water in your body and your cells, leaving you tired and sore and causing headaches and other symptoms.

Drinking water throughout the day is a great way to rehydrate your body. Consuming a lot of water throughout the day is best to promote recovery within the body. You could do more harm than good if you try to go about your typical day without rehydrating.

Get Some Rest

When you have a hangover trying to exert yourself is likely to make things worse or leave you more tired than usual. That’s why most experts suggest resting to help you speed up your recovery from a hangover.

Sleep is the most effective option, but even if you do not feel tired, lying in a comfortable position and resting your body is another way to help yourself recover more quickly.

Eat Carbohydrates

One of the most common symptoms of a hangover is nausea and vomiting. These symptoms are caused by alcohol irritating the lining of the stomach. Over time alcohol causes the lining of the stomach to become inflamed; when this happens, it leads to nausea and the potential of vomiting.

Your body needs energy to move, and that energy comes from carbs. You must also be careful what you eat, though, as many foods will only aggravate the stomach. However, mild or plain foods like soup, crackers, toast, and pasta can help ease the stomach and give your body the energy it needs.

If you are struggling to keep food down, drinking ginger ale or eating some ginger is known to help settle the stomach, making it easier to hold food down. Try to have breakfast and not go without eating too long because depriving yourself can make you feel weak and interfere with healing and recovery.

Over the Counter Medications

If you continue to suffer from a headache or nausea and vomiting, you can use over-the-counter medications to treat those symptoms. Ibuprofen is great for dealing with the pain of a headache. Anti-nausea medications can combat nausea, while antacids can reduce stomach acid production to help protect our stomach lining from further irritation.

If you have other symptoms, you can usually find an over-the-counter remedy that can help relieve discomfort while your body recovers.

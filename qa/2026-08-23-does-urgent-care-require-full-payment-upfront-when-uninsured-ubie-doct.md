Uninsured patients are not always required to pay in full upfront at urgent care centers. Many facilities offer flexible options, including payment plans, sliding-scale fees based on income, self-pay discounts, or financial assistance programs. Policies vary by location, so it's best to call ahead and ask about pricing, upfront costs, and available financial support before your visit.

Explanation

Flavanols are a type of natural compound found in foods like cocoa, tea, and some fruits. They are part of a larger group called flavonoids. Flavanols are known for their potential health benefits, especially for the heart and blood vessels.

Benefits of Flavanols:

Improved Blood Flow: Flavanols can help make blood vessels wider, which improves blood flow and can lower blood pressure.

Heart Health: By improving blood flow, flavanols may help protect the heart from diseases.

Antioxidant Effects: They also have antioxidant properties, which means they help protect the body from damage caused by harmful molecules called free radicals.
